🎙️ How Voice Entry Works

Hold the mic and speak, starting with "Item" for the name & description, then "Tag" followed by the amount — e.g. "Item Coke one point five liter bottle Tag 65". Tap into any field first (like Barcode), then say "Continue" to dictate straight into it at the cursor — e.g. "Continue 4800001234567".

⌨️ How Manual Entry Works

You can also hold the mic below and speak — "Item" fills the name, "Tag" fills the price, or tap into any field (like Description or Barcode) and say "Continue" to dictate straight into it.
